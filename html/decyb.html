The diagram shows the distance sailed over time (upper part, upper curve), the distance made good (upper part lower curve), and the average speed (lower part) of all participants of a specific race provided by YB. The distance sailed is calculated by the actual trackpoints of each boat.

Add the URL parameter "?race=NAME" where NAME is the name of the race, e.g. ggr2022, or ogr2023. The smaller the area between the two curves the closer does a participant stay to the intended race course. It could be seen as a perfomance measure. Hover over the participants' names to display the curves. Click on the names to make the curves sticky. The blue line within the map shows the day-night terminator.
